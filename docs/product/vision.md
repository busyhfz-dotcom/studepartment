# Studepartment Product Vision

Studepartment is an AI-powered, privacy-first scientific intelligence and connection platform for medical and biomedical research.

## Mission

Reduce the friction involved in finding the right researcher, laboratory, institution, position, grant, or collaborator.

## Product principles

1. Facilitation over engagement.
2. Relevance over volume.
3. Trust over popularity.
4. Private, permission-based communication.
5. Explainable AI recommendations.
6. Scientific data provenance and freshness.

## What Studepartment is not

- A public social feed.
- A follower competition.
- A public researcher ranking system.
- A general-purpose messaging network.
- An advertising-surveillance product.

## North-star outcome

Meaningful Scientific Connections: a relevant discovery that progresses into a useful scientific conversation, application, collaboration, or research opportunity.
