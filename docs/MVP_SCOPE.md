# MVP Scope and Success Criteria

## Primary user

An active medical researcher who wants to discover relevant people and opportunities without maintaining another social media presence or sending generic cold outreach.

## Core jobs to be done

1. Represent my scientific identity accurately enough to be discovered in context.
2. Find researchers/labs whose work genuinely intersects with mine.
3. Find opportunities worth evaluating and understand why they may fit.
4. Request an introduction without exposing either party to unrestricted messaging.
5. Control what parts of my identity and contact information are visible.

## MVP capabilities

### Scientific identity

Profile, institution, research topics, methods, current focus, publication references, verification state and visibility state.

### Discovery

Researcher/entity search, structured filters, semantic candidate retrieval and human-readable relevance explanations.

### Opportunity intelligence

Normalized opportunity records, deadlines, source provenance, saved items and profile-aware explanation.

### Introductions

Requester context, recipient consent, state transitions (pending/accepted/declined/withdrawn), rate limits and auditability.

## Explicitly not MVP

- follower/following graph;
- public popularity scores;
- unrestricted direct messaging;
- automated candidate rejection or hiring eligibility decisions;
- institution-wide analytics built on private individual behavior;
- broad all-discipline taxonomy before the initial oncology/immunology/clinical-research domain is working well.

## Product metrics

Prefer outcome metrics over engagement metrics:

- percentage of searches that lead to a profile/opportunity judged relevant;
- introduction acceptance rate adjusted for abuse/spam controls;
- opportunity saves/applications after explanation review;
- recommendation false-positive reports;
- time required to reach a useful scientific next step;
- verified profile completion and data freshness.
